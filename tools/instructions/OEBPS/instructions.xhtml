How to use your Kindle

Press the button beside the screen to turn to the next page of these instructions.

Reading a book

Your books stand on the Book Shelf. Tap the title of a book to open it.

To turn the page, press one of the buttons beside the screen. You can also slide your finger across the page: to the left for the next page, or to the right to go back.

The Kindle remembers your place, so a book always opens where you left off.

The reading menu

Tap anywhere on the page to bring up the reading menu. Tap beside it to put it away.

Change Text Size makes the words Bigger or Smaller.

Turn on light or Adjust light makes the screen Brighter or Darker, or turns the light off.

Find a place takes you back to the beginning, or helps you find a page you were reading. It asks whether your page is Before or After the one on screen, and each answer gets closer. Tap Done when you see your page.

Stop for now puts the Kindle to sleep. Press the power button to wake it.

Open another book takes you back to the Book Shelf. Your place in this book is kept.

Finishing a book

When you turn past the last page, the Kindle asks what to do with the book.

Put back on shelf keeps the book, ready to start again from the beginning.

Throw away removes the book from the Kindle. It asks you first, and Cancel keeps it.

Read again now starts the book again.

Go back to last page returns to the end of the book.

Finding a book on the shelf

The books stand in order of their titles, as in a library. When there are too many for one shelf, the letters at the top say which books this shelf holds. Tap an arrow to see the shelf beside it.

Look up a book finds a book for you. Tap the letter it starts with, tap Read lately for the books you have opened most recently, or tap Type a name to type part of its title or the author’s name.

Throwing a book away

To remove a book you no longer want, keep your finger on its title on the shelf for four seconds. The Kindle asks whether to throw it away. Tap Yes, it's rubbish to remove it, or Cancel to keep it.

Getting a new book

Tap Book Shop at the bottom of the screen, then Find a new book. The Kindle turns on the Wi‑Fi to look for books for you.

The battery

When the battery is running low, a message at the bottom of the page says the Kindle needs to be charged. Plug it in, and you can keep reading while it charges.

Settings

Tap Settings at the bottom of the screen.

Instructions opens this book.

Check for updates looks for improvements to the Kindle. It updates by itself too, so you never need to.

Advanced is for whoever looks after the Kindle for you.

Tap Open another book in the reading menu to go back to your books.
